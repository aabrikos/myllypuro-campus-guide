How navigation works

Choose a room and review its route. Blue marks the start and red marks the destination area. Start camera uses your initial facing direction and estimated movement to point to each bend. On iPhone, each detected step counts as about 0.65 m. If the arrow drifts, exit and restart at the blue dot. Practice walk lets you hold the turn and walk buttons anywhere.

Paths are estimates from the 2015 drawing. Current doors, room uses and walking distances still need an on-site check.
