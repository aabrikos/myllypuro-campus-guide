# Presentation and Q&A
## Live demonstration
1. Introduce the problem. A student knows the classroom name but struggles to orient a floor plan in a corridor.
2. Show the starting-point map. Search for Demo classroom and open its route from the info stand.
3. Start Practice walk. Rotate toward the arrow and walk. Show the arrow responding and the destination confirmation. For the camera demonstration, use Safari on the phone and enable camera and motion access.
4. Explain the limit. The model starts at an assumed position. Physical accuracy depends on real routes, initial heading and step length.
## Questions to prepare
**Why one floor?** It makes the navigation problem and the starting pose concrete enough to demonstrate and test.
**How does the arrow turn?** The app compares the bearing to the next checkpoint with the estimated phone heading. As the visitor reaches a checkpoint the next one becomes the target.
**What did AI contribute?** Planning, interface and route implementation, browser checks, and deployment. Demonstrate the choices you personally tested and the feedback you gave.
**What has been verified?** Browser checks covered the directory and simulated route arrivals. A real walk on each route is still needed to check its accuracy. The student tried an earlier camera prototype on iPhone 15.
**Why 51 destinations?** Only drawing labels with a traceable approach were retained. The dated drawing leaves uncertainty about current rooms and doors.
